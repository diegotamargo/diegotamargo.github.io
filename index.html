Early Fault Detection in Wind Turbines

Will a turbine component fail within the next 14 days, while the turbine is still running normally?

I trained a classifier on 10-minute SCADA readings from five onshore turbines at an EDP wind farm in Portugal (CARE To Compare dataset). Labels come from the farm's logbook of confirmed failures.

Gearbox failures are the easiest to see coming (0.98 ROC-AUC); generator failures are a coin flip (0.49).

How it works
  • Each sensor is turned into a smoothed z-score against that turbine's own healthy baseline, plus its trend.
  • Positive labels cover the 14 days before a confirmed failure, while the turbine still runs.
  • GroupKFold by failure event: each fold is tested on failures the model never saw.

Energy Mix & CO₂ Analysis

Does a country's fuel mix explain its CO₂ emissions, and which model forecasts them best?

I rebuilt an old statistics course project with Our World in Data records for 79 countries, cleaned in a four-layer DuckDB pipeline. Countries are clustered by fuel mix with k-means, and four forecasting models compete for each country under rolling-origin cross-validation.

The fuel mix sets how dirty each unit of energy is, but emissions per person follow how much energy a country uses (r = 0.75), not its carbon intensity (r = 0.01).

How it works
  • Four SQL layers in DuckDB, from raw CSV to a per-country feature matrix.
  • k-means on fuel shares alone, so emissions stay out and can be tested afterwards.
  • ARIMA, ETS, drift and naive compared by MASE over ten rolling ten-year test windows.
