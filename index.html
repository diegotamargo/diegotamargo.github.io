01STATISTICS

Energy Mix & CO₂ Analysis

A statistical study of how a country’s energy mix relates to its emissions and its economic output, across 10 economies from 2000 to 2020 on Our World in Data figures. Multiple regression, t-tests and confidence intervals in Excel; K-means clustering and ARIMA forecasting in R. Coal comes out as the strongest single driver of emissions, consistently outweighing renewable adoption, and the clustering separates the countries into four profiles: a Western European core, the US as a high-consumption outlier, France as a nuclear one, and China with Bulgaria as high-carbon industrializing economies.

02PREDICTIVE MAINT.

Wind Turbine Failure Predictor

Early fault detection on real SCADA data: will a component fail within the next 14 days, while the turbine is still running normally? Trained on the EDP open wind farm — 5 onshore turbines in Portugal, 10-minute SCADA plus a logbook of confirmed failures. Every sensor is recast as its deviation from that turbine’s own healthy baseline, and validation uses GroupKFold by failure event, so no fold is ever tested on a failure it has seen. The finding worth reporting is that failures are not equally predictable: gearbox failures are caught reliably about two weeks ahead (ROC-AUC 0.99), while generator and hydraulic ones leave almost no fingerprint at that horizon. The whole project is one notebook, pandas and scikit-learn only.

03GEOSPATIAL / CV

OrtophotoSegmentation

Turns a georeferenced PNOA orthophoto into a CAD drawing. A U-Net (EfficientNet-B4, OpenEarthMap weights) segments buildings and roads tile by tile and blends the overlap halos so seams come out as gradients instead of steps. The masks are cleaned with morphology expressed in metres, then vectorized into closed polylines: buildings orthogonally regularized, roads as closed kerb rings with real curves carried as arcs, so intersections close by construction and blocks come out as holes. It exports DXF, GeoPackage and a QC preview in the original UTM coordinates, splitting a full sheet into boxes so a 25 cm MTN25 tile fits in memory. Runs on Colab or on local COG/GeoTIFF files. MIT licensed.

04RAG SYSTEM

NormaINGECA

A search tool that lets engineers ask questions about compliance and regulatory documents in plain language instead of digging through PDFs. Built on LlamaIndex, it pairs BM25 keyword search with vector retrieval and merges the two with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, backed by a FAISS index. Nothing runs locally: embeddings, reranking and generation all go through NVIDIA’s developer APIs — Nemotron for embedding and reranking, Llama 8B for the answer — which keeps it light enough for ordinary office machines. It’s now installed on more than 50 workstations as a standalone Windows app.
